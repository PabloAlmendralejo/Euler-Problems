Every number can be broken down into prime factors. So we start dividing `N` by the smallest primes and keep going. But we don’t need to check forever, if you think about any number as a product of two factors, one of them has to be smaller than or equal to the square root of the number. That means once we have divided out all the factors up to √N, whatever is left has to be prime, and that’s actually the largest prime factor.
